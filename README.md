# ECHO — See Beyond the Answer

> **"Don't just show me what happened. Show me what I'm missing."**

**ECHO** is an AI-assisted critical thinking and evidence intelligence platform designed to deconstruct arguments, separate facts from assumptions, uncover missing stakeholder perspectives, and stress-test claims through interactive epistemic challenge models.

---

## 🌟 Product Vision

People encounter bold assertions daily across media, workplaces, academic research, and policy debates. Too often, people accept or reject conclusions without understanding:
- **Whether the claim is empirically supported** or merely correlates with broader trends.
- **Which assumptions are hidden** and acting as unverified load-bearing pillars.
- **Which counter-evidence or systemic frictions** are being minimized or omitted.
- **Which stakeholder perspectives are being silenced or overlooked**.
- **What happens to the entire conclusion when a single assumption is challenged**.

The objective of ECHO is **not to tell users what to think**, but to **help users think with structural clarity**.

## 🎯 Curated Master Showcase

### **Claim:** *"AI will replace all programmers in two years."*

ECHO deconstructs this intense technological debate:
1. **Facts**: Validates that generative models can author syntactically valid code, pass competitive benchmarks (HumanEval/LeetCode), and accelerate routine boilerplate.
2. **Hidden Assumptions Exposed**:
   - Assumes software engineering equals typing syntax rather than systems architecture, requirements elicitation, and risk management.
   - Assumes global enterprise and legal institutions can adopt autonomous unverified code within 24 months without human liability sign-off.
   - Assumes foreign code verification is faster than human authoring with contextual awareness.
3. **Challenging Evidence**:
   - **Time Allocation Studies**: Empirical software engineering studies show only 20–30% of engineering hours involve writing code; 70% is requirements, review, and telemetry.
   - **The Jevons Paradox**: Historical computing revolutions (Compilers in 1957, SQL in 1980s) dramatically increased demand for human developers by making software cheaper.
   - **Enterprise Tech Debt**: Millions of lines of legacy systems (COBOL, banking mainframes) lack formal specifications.
4. **Missing Stakeholder Perspectives**:
   - *The Formal Verification Scientist*: Highlights mathematical limits of stochastic token generation without formal proofs.
   - *The Economic Skeptic*: Spotlights insurance underwriters and legal liability for catastrophic outages.
   - *The Technology Historian*: Details 70 years of "death of programmer" panics following new abstraction layers.
   - *The Labor Humanist*: Warns against cutting junior apprenticeships, threatening future senior developer intuition.
   - *The Contrarian*: Argues code is liability, and the future bottleneck is deleting code and constraining scope.
5. **Interactive Challenge Ripple**:
   - Challenge the assumption *"Software engineering is typing syntax"*, and watch the overall verdict shift from `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` to `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

Additional preloaded investigations:
- *"Social media is making students less intelligent."*
- *"A four-day workweek increases productivity."*
- *"Banning smartphones improves academic performance."*
- Plus **any custom claim** entered by the user via the dynamic local intelligence engine!

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend Core**: React 18 / 19, TypeScript
- **Build & Bundling**: Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS (custom dark intelligence theme `#06080F`, electric cyan `#00F2FE`, restrained violet `#8B5CF6`)
- **Iconography**: Lucide React
- **Knowledge Graph**: Custom interactive SVG canvas (dynamic layouts, zooming, panning, node highlighting, accessible linear view)
- **Data Persistence**: Browser LocalStorage (with rename, delete, reset, and export)
- **Zero API Keys Required**: 100% deterministic, offline-ready local intelligence simulation.

## ⚖️ Epistemic Transparency & Integrity

ECHO upholds strict ethical guardrails:
- **No Fabricated Citations**: Illustrative benchmark data is explicitly labeled as such; user-provided inputs are clearly distinguished.
- **No Manufactured Consensus**: Never assigns arbitrary pseudo-scientific percentage scores. All confidence ratings are qualitative (`Strongly Supported`, `Partially Supported`, `Mixed Evidence`, `Insufficient Evidence`, `Not Currently Verifiable`).
- **No Partisan Bias**: Arguments are deconstructed according to logic, assumptions, and counter-perspectives, not ideological agendas.
